# Niche page growth strategy

## Caption / hashtags
> Want to grow a viral page from zero? Pick one niche and go deep.
> 
> The strategy starts with a single topic. Take “Hair Wolf” as the example. One page, one subject: hair care and grooming. Launch it on Instagram and YouTube together.
> 
> Then do the research:
> 
> Find the top beauty and grooming channels.
> List their 10 best-performing hair videos.
> Cover subtopics like shaving, hair length, styling and hair removal.
> 
> Those videos already proved what people want to watch. Make content on those exact topics.
> 
> Then build every video around two metrics:
> 
> Average view duration. Structure the video so it builds up and pays off at the very end. Viewers stay till the last second.
> Shareability. People share for two reasons. They send it to a friend who relates to the same identity, or they repost it to build their own personal brand.
> 
> Design each video for one of those two shares.
> 
> Don’t guess what works. Copy what’s proven, then make it shareable.
