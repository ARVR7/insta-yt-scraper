> How to get this?

Steps (general approach from Oracle's public docs; not tested here): 1) Sign up at oracle.com/cloud/free with a valid email and a payment card (used for identity verification; Always Free resources are not billed, but pay-as-you-go upgrades can be). 2) Choose your home region carefully; Always Free resources are tied to it and it cannot be changed later. 3) In the console create a Compute instance, pick shape VM.Standard.A1.Flex marked Always Free eligible, set up to 2 OCPU and 12GB RAM, choose an Ubuntu or Oracle Linux image, add your SSH public key. 4) If you get 'Out of host capacity', try again later, another availability domain, or a smaller shape; capacity is the commonly reported obstacle. 5) Open only the ports you need, and keep the instance used, since Oracle can reclaim idle or over-limit free resources.
