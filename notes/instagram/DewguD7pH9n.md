# Tanmay Bhat on building a viral content page

## Caption / hashtags
> What if you could build a viral content page by studying what people already want to watch?
> 
> In a conversation on The Barbershop with Shantanu, Tanmay Bhat breaks down a practical strategy for building a content brand from scratch.
> 
> Imagine launching a page called “Hair Wolf.” Instead of guessing what to post, start by studying the top beauty and grooming channels. Identify their most popular hair-related videos, from shaving and hairstyles to hair length, design, and removal. These topics reveal what audiences are already interested in watching.
> 
> But finding the right topic is only the beginning.
> 
> Tanmay highlights two important questions: how can you increase Average View Duration (AVD), and why would someone share your video? Would they send it to a friend with similar interests, or share it on their profile to build their own identity?
> 
> The lesson? Don't create content based only on what you want to say. Study audience demand, understand viewer behaviour, and make every video worth watching and sharing.
