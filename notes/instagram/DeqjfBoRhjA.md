### Ingredients

- 800 g chicken breast, raw, diced
- 150 g red bell pepper, capsicum
- 150 g yellow bell pepper, capsicum
- 150 g green bell pepper, capsicum
- 100 g red onion
- 40 g spice mix, fajita seasoning, approximated with the generic spice mix row
- 15 ml lime juice (15.5 g)
- 10 g olive oil, or cooking spray
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

### Method

1. Dice the chicken, capsicums and red onion.
2. Put everything in the air fryer basket or an air-fryer-safe tray.
3. Add the fajita seasoning, lime juice and olive oil and mix until evenly coated.
4. Air fry at 190°C for about 15 to 20 minutes, tossing halfway, until the chicken reaches 75°C inside.
5. Divide into four servings; optionally serve with rice, wraps or potatoes.

### Tips

- Serve with rice, wraps or potatoes for extra carbohydrates; they are not in the macros.
