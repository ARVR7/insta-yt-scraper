# TOFU MOFU BOFU content for brand deals

## Caption / hashtags
> Check description for more questions 
> 
> Not all views are equal. Here's the difference between TOFU, MOFU and BOFU - and which one brands actually pay the most for.
> 
> TOFU (top of funnel) is awareness content. Broad, relatable, easiest to make. Example: "5 things to do before sleep to lose weight." Anyone scrolling stops for it, so the views are big - but there's no buying intent behind them.
> 
> MOFU (middle of funnel) is consideration content. The viewer already has the problem and wants to understand it. Example: "Why eating this affects your protein - and how that helps you build muscle and lose fat." Fewer views, more trust.
> 
> BOFU (bottom of funnel) is decision content. The viewer is ready to act. Example: "How I lost 5 kg - exactly what I did, how you can do it too, and the product that made it easier." Smallest views, highest value.
> 
> The quick test for any video: what can the viewer DO right after watching? Just know something = TOFU. Understand their problem better = MOFU. Ready to buy = BOFU.
> 
> Brands pay at every stage through, but the money is moving to BOFU - because that's the stage where the wallet is already open. 
> 
> TOFU is the easiest content to make and some brand pay for this too whe they are trying to achieve awareness . BOFU is the content that gets you paid since brand gets conversion.
