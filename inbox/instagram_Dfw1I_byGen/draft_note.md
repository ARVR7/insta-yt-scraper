> The people getting real results do it differently. They don't write better prompts. They build loops.
> 
> A loop doesn't hope the AI gets it right the first time. It gives the AI a way to check its own work and keep improving until the output is good enough to ship.
> 
> Every strong loop has four parts:
> 
> 1️⃣ Context: Who the AI is, who it's serving, and what "done" looks like.
> 2️⃣ Process: The exact steps to follow, in order, every time.
> 3️⃣ Self-review: A critic step where the AI grades its draft against clear criteria.
> 4️⃣ Stop rule: A measurable finish line, like "scores 9/10 or higher" or "under 200 words", so it knows when to stop.
>
