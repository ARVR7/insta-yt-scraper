AIR FRYER CHICKEN FAJITAS!

MACROS PER SERVE (Approx.)

Calories: 320
Protein: 51g
Carbs: 14g
Fat: 6g

INGREDIENTS (4 SERVINGS)

* 800g raw chicken breast, diced
* 150g red capsicum
* 150g yellow capsicum
* 150g green capsicum
* 100g red onion
* 40g fajita seasoning
* 15ml fresh lime juice
* 10g olive oil or cooking spray
* Salt and pepper to taste

METHOD

1. Dice your chicken, capsicums and red onion.
2. Add everything directly into your air fryer basket or air fryer-safe tray.
3. Add the fajita seasoning, lime juice and olive oil, then mix everything together until evenly coated.
4. Air fry at 190°C for approximately 15–20 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the chicken is cooked through (75°C internally).
5. Divide into four servings and enjoy!

OPTIONAL: Serve with rice, wraps or potatoes for some additional carbohydrates. These aren’t included in the macros above.

High protein, low calorie, packed with flavour and minimal washing up!
