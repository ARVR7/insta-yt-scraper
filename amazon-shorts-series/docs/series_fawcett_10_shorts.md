# Series: "The Man Who Walked Into the Amazon" — 10 Shorts, 16-18s each

Status: DRAFT for approval. Nothing rendered yet.
Facts marked [VERIFY] must be checked against primary sources before posting. Do not publish them unchecked.

## Series rules (apply to every part)
- Top badge: "PART N/10" in the top-safe area (y 200-260), same position and style every part. The caption and title also say "Part N/10".
- 0:00 stakes: a "DAYS MISSING" counter (starts at 0 in Part 1, continues from the previous part's number in later parts).
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, one on-screen line of text per beat (no voiceover; captions plus synthesized score).
- Hidden detail at 0:11 in every part: a tiny pottery-shard icon. The pause prompt says "Pause at 0:11". Collecting all 10 shards is the series loop.
- Ends on a cliffhanger line plus "PART N+1 NEXT". Part 10 ends by pointing back to Part 1 so the series loops.
- Look: dark jungle night palette, flat vector silhouettes, parallax layers, glowing orange accent, neon cyan UI. Reuses the Shorts kit (timer, stamps, grayscale freeze, 130 bpm score).
- Safe zones: top 200px, right 120px, bottom 300px stay empty of text.
