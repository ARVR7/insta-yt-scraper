# Hoaxes and misleading claims

Claims judged `hoax` or `misleading`, with the evidence. Newest first. See `.claude/skills/update-registries/SKILL.md` for the entry format.

### "Free AI studio makes lacs of rupees every month on autopilot"  (2026-10-17)
- **Verdict:** misleading
- **What it is:** the reel's headline claim that a free, fully automated 24/7 AI YouTube pipeline produces "thousands" to "lacs" of rupees a month with no editor, voice actor or subscription.
- **Why it is misleading:**
  - The tool components are real and free (see `verified.md`), but no income evidence is shown: no channel, no analytics, no payout.
  - YouTube's inauthentic-content policy targets exactly this model (mass-produced, template-based, AI-generated videos) and can remove channels from the Partner Program ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
  - Free tiers have rate limits (Gemini, Pexels), so "24/7 for free" is capped.
  - The guide is handed out only to people who comment a keyword, a common lead-generation funnel.
- **Verdict scope:** the software may be real; the earnings claim is unsubstantiated and unlikely as stated.
- **Evidence:** [YouTube Help: YPP policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [Pexels API limits](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/47677890260761-Is-the-Pexels-API-free-to-use), [Gemini rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits)
- **Source note:** [notes/instagram/DeToMLxtdSV.md](../notes/instagram/DeToMLxtdSV.md)
