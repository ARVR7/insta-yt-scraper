# Hoaxes and misleading claims

Claims judged `hoax` or `misleading`, with the evidence. Newest first. See `.claude/skills/update-registries/SKILL.md` for the entry format.

### "No other tool runs MiniMax H3 on 8 GB"  (2026-10-17)
- **Verdict:** misleading
- **What it is:** the reel's claim that FreeVideo is the only way to run this on an 8 GB card, with other tools needing 32 GB or more.
- **Why it is misleading:** the "out of memory" comparison is the project's own README chart against the dense H3 model. Independent guides say a quantised stock H3 with CPU offload is possible on 8 GB, just slow, and one puts the floor at a 6 GB card. FreeVideo is a good option, not the only one.
- **Evidence:** [ZimaSpace](https://shop.zimaspace.com/blogs/tech-ai-hub/running-minimax-h3-locally-hardware-comfyui-and-a-self-hosted-ai-video-setup), [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/minimax-h3-run-locally-guide), [GitHub: FlashML-org/FreeVideo](https://github.com/FlashML-org/FreeVideo)
- **Source note:** [notes/instagram/DeSArmKIhid.md](../notes/instagram/DeSArmKIhid.md)

### "Comment KATANA for the free Higgsfield Claude MCP / setup link"  (2026-10-17)
- **Verdict:** misleading
- **What it is:** a comment-for-DM offer of a "free" Higgsfield Claude MCP and setup link.
- **Why it is misleading:**
  - Katana uses credits from a Higgsfield plan and "an active Higgsfield subscription is required"; the blog mentions no free option.
  - There is nothing to send: the connector URL (`https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp`) is published on Higgsfield's pages, so the comment funnel only collects engagement.
  - The reel is tagged to @higgsfield.ai and reads as a promotion; the demo shows playback only, not a generation.
- **Verdict scope:** the product is real; the "free" framing and the implied one-prompt result are not supported.
- **Evidence:** [Higgsfield blog: Katana](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-katana), [Higgsfield MCP](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp)
- **Source note:** [notes/instagram/DegLPFbTBpi.md](../notes/instagram/DegLPFbTBpi.md)

### "Free AI studio makes lacs of rupees every month on autopilot"  (2026-10-17)
- **Verdict:** misleading
- **What it is:** the reel's headline claim that a free, fully automated 24/7 AI YouTube pipeline produces "thousands" to "lacs" of rupees a month with no editor, voice actor or subscription.
- **Why it is misleading:**
  - The tool components are real and free (see `verified.md`), but no income evidence is shown: no channel, no analytics, no payout.
  - YouTube's inauthentic-content policy targets exactly this model (mass-produced, template-based, AI-generated videos) and can remove channels from the Partner Program ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
  - Free tiers have rate limits (Gemini, Pexels), so "24/7 for free" is capped.
  - The guide is handed out only to people who comment a keyword, a common lead-generation funnel.
- **Verdict scope:** the software may be real; the earnings claim is unsubstantiated and unlikely as stated.
- **Evidence:** [YouTube Help: YPP policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [Pexels API limits](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/47677890260761-Is-the-Pexels-API-free-to-use), [Gemini rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits)
- **Source note:** [notes/instagram/DeToMLxtdSV.md](../notes/instagram/DeToMLxtdSV.md)
